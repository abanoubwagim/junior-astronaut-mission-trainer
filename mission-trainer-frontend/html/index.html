Mission briefing 🌌

Guide the crew through your mission days. Each round uses game-value supplies, and your earlier repairs can change what happens next.

🎯 Your objective

Keep Power, Water, and Food above zero, then finish Day 4 with a ready shelter. Daily needs are used before each decision. Choose a plan that helps today and leaves the crew ready for tomorrow.

🎮 How to play

Read the situation and science note. Select one choice, review its trade-off, and confirm it. Mission Control calculates every supply change.
