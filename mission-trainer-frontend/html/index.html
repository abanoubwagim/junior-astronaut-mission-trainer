✨ DAY 1 EVENT

Dust on the solar array 🌞

The rover spots dust on the solar array, and mission control asks the crew to prepare for a possible solar-weather alert. Use the work window to boost power later or prepare the shelter.

ℹ️ SCIENCE SIGNAL

Lunar dust can cling to exposed surfaces and make solar-array power harder to maintain. NASA has an Orion plan to use stored supplies as a shelter during a solar-particle event.
